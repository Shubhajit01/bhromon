# Product

## Register

Brand

## Users

Independent travellers planning a self-guided trip around their own interests, pace, and sense of discovery.

## Product Purpose

Bhromon helps a traveller turn a personal trip idea into a journey they can confidently make their own.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and curious. Nature-led, personal, and quietly optimistic.

## Anti-references

Generic AI spectacle, abstract geometric imagery, and busy marketing surfaces that compete with the traveller's idea.

## Design Principles

1. Let the journey feel personal before it feels automated.
2. Put the traveller's next thought at the centre of the experience.
3. Use natural imagery to create anticipation, not distraction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, focus, and motion expectations so every traveller can begin planning with confidence.
